Modern Ruthenian

An archaic East Slavic language

Overview

This language is an "a posteriori" conlang created just because. It has no goals or uses, but is simply one of my many natlang-inspired projects created simply for the sake of creating.

Phonology (Consonants)

The phonology of the language is almost entirely identical to that of other East Slavic languages, as they all have very few differences from one another. It is usually considered to be closest to that of Ukrainian, as that is also Modern Ruthenian's closest living relative.

Phonology (Vowels)

Modern Ruthenian, just like all of its relatives (excpet for Russian), has a pretty simple vowel system.

Grammar: Noun Declensions

The language features a robust system of seven cases (Nominative, Genitive, Dative, Accusative, Instrumental, Locative, Vocative), three genders (Masculine, Feminine, Neuter), and three numbers (Singular, Dual, Plural). Below are the main declensions:
